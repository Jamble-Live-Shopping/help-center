# Schedule a live

## What you'll learn

This guide shows how to create and schedule a live on Jamble: cover, title, description, category, date and time, repeat, and pinned notes. You'll also see how to edit, postpone, or delete a live you already scheduled.

## Before you start

You need:
- An approved seller account on Jamble
- Email, phone, address, and payment method filled in on your profile. If something is missing, the app asks you to complete it before opening the form
- Camera and microphone access allowed for the app

## Where to start

In the app, open your profile, tap the **+** button, and choose **Agendar uma Live** (Schedule a Live). You can also schedule from your computer in the Seller Hub, with the **Agendar Live** (Schedule Live) button.

The **Criar Live** (Create Live) form opens on a single screen. Fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required.

## Fill in the form

### Cover (required)

Tap **Adicionar imagem de capa** (Add a cover image) and pick an option:
- **Importar da biblioteca** (Import from library): uses a photo from your phone
- **Explorar capas** (Explore covers): searches a ready-made image inside the app

The cover shows in a vertical format on live cards. We recommend a vertical image in a **2:3** ratio, with the most important part in the center, because the edges can be cropped on some screens. A photo of the real products in the live usually attracts more buyers than a generic image.

### Detalhes da Live (Live details)

- **Título** (Title, required): this is what buyers see first. Be specific, for example "Pokémon TCG, boosters and single cards"
- **Descrição** (Description, optional): one or two sentences about what you will sell

The field itself shows how many characters you have left.

### Category (required)

Tap **Categoria** (Category) and choose one. The category helps buyers find your live in filters. Pick the one that covers most of your items.

### Date and time (required)

Tap **Selecione sua data** (Select your date), choose the day on the calendar, set the **Hora** (Time), and tap **Confirmar** (Confirm). You can't choose a time that has already passed.

Two of your lives can't be too close to each other. If the time you pick is close to another scheduled live, the app asks you to choose another date.

### Repetir Live (Repeat Live, optional)

If you always go live at the same time, turn on **Repetir Live** to create several lives at once:
- **A cada** (Every): choose the interval (Dia, Semana, or Mês, meaning Day, Week, or Month) and how many units between lives
- **Termina** (Ends): choose **Na Data** (On date, then set the **Data de término**, End date) or a fixed number, such as **Após 3 Lives** (After 3 lives)

The final button shows how many lives will be created, for example **Criar 5 lives** (Create 5 lives). Repeat can only be set up when you create the live. After that, each live in the series is edited on its own.

### Notas fixadas (Pinned notes, optional)

In the **Notas fixadas** field (on Android, **Fixar Anotações**), write a short notice for viewers, such as the rules of the live or the time of a special lot. The field shows the character limit.

The notes are pinned in the live chat for viewers. Anyone who wants to read them again can open **Notas** (Notes) in the live menu.

Only use notes for true, current information. Don't advertise discount codes that don't exist in the app.

## Confirm the schedule

Once the required fields are filled in, the **Criar Live** (Create Live) button becomes available. Tap it to schedule.

Next, the app suggests **Adicionar Produtos** (Add Products) to the live. You can add them now or tap **Faço depois** (I'll do it later) and prepare your products calmly before you start.

The live then shows on your profile and to buyers. Your followers may get a notice when the live starts, depending on the notifications they turned on in the app.

## Allowed content in the title, description, and notes

The title, description, and notes go through an automatic filter. Phone numbers, links, profiles on other networks, or requests to deal outside Jamble are not allowed. If the text is blocked, the app shows a warning and the live isn't saved until you change the text.

## Edit a scheduled live

Open the scheduled live from your profile, tap the menu, and choose **Editar live** (Edit live). You can change the cover, title, description, category, date and time, and the pinned notes. Tap **Editar live** at the bottom of the form to save.

Editing is only possible before the live starts.

### Postpone or bring forward

Moving a live to an earlier time is free. Moving it later has a limit per live. When you reach the limit, the app shows **Limite de adiamentos atingido** (Postpone limit reached) and won't accept a new later date. In the Seller Hub, the edit form shows how many postpones you have left.

## Delete a scheduled live

Open the scheduled live, tap the menu, and choose **Excluir** (Delete). Confirm at the question **Tem certeza de que deseja excluir esta live?** (Are you sure you want to delete this live?). Deleting can't be undone.

## Important tips

- **Schedule at busy times.** Evenings and weekends usually bring more buyers
- **Build a habit.** A fixed live, like every Thursday at 8 pm, helps your buyers come back
- **Add products ahead of time.** A live with products already listed gets more attention
- **Use the notes for rules.** A pinned notice saves you from answering the same question many times
- **Practice before your first live.** On the live screen, before you start, you can test the camera and products with no one watching in **Modo de prática** (Practice mode; on Android, **Modo Teste**)

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I schedule two lives at the same time?**
No. Your lives need a gap between them. If the time is taken, the app asks for another date.

**Can I change the date after scheduling?**
Yes, with **Editar live** (Edit live). Bringing it forward is free. Postponing has a limit per live.

**Can I edit the notes during the live?**
No. The title, description, notes, and date can only be changed before the live starts.

**Can I add products after scheduling?**
Yes. You can add, edit, and arrange products at any time before and during the live.

**Why isn't the Criar Live button available?**
Check that you added the cover, title, category, and date. If you turned on **Repetir Live** (Repeat Live), also choose when the repeat ends.

**What if I'm late?**
Start the live as soon as you can. If the time has passed and the live didn't start, you can pick a new date on the live screen with **Atrasado? Reviva esta Live** (Late? Revive this live).
