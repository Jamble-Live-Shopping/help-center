# Seller Referral Program

## What you will learn

How to invite other people to sell on Jamble, when a referral counts and where to follow your referrals.

## How it works

1. You share your invite link or code with someone who does not sell on Jamble yet.
2. They sign up and are approved as a seller.
3. The referral is completed when they make their first sale within the deadline, to a buyer other than you.
4. You both get a reward.

The reward amounts and conditions always show on the app's referral screen, under **Indicar vendedores** (refer sellers). They can change during special events, such as double dates.

## Who can refer sellers

To refer new sellers, you need to be a seller with a sales history on Jamble.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Open referrals

In the app, open the referral area and choose **Indicar vendedores**. On a computer, open **Indicações** in the Seller Hub and choose **Indicar Vendedor**.

### Step 2: Share your link

Send your link or code through WhatsApp, Instagram or any other app. The link already includes your code.

### Step 3: They sign up as a seller

They use your code, sign up as a seller and need to be approved. Then they have a set time to make their first sale, shown on the referral screen.

### Step 4: The referral is completed

When the first sale happens within the deadline, the referral shows as completed and the reward is released.

## How to follow your referrals

Under **Suas Indicações** (your referrals), you see:

- **Indicações Pendentes** (pending): people who used your code and are still within the deadline
- **Indicações Finalizadas** (completed): completed referrals
- **Indicações Expiradas** (expired): people who did not make their first sale in time

## Important rules

- **Only for people who do not sell on Jamble yet**
- **The first sale must be to someone else.** Purchases you make yourself do not count
- **Fake referrals are cancelled,** such as accounts created only to get the reward, and can lead to account restrictions

## Frequently asked questions

**How much do I earn per referral?**
The amounts and conditions show on the app's referral screen, under **Indicar vendedores**.

**How long do they have to make their first sale?**
The deadline shows on the app's referral screen.

**I can't refer sellers. Why?**
To refer new sellers, you need a sales history on Jamble. If you have questions about your account, contact support.

**They only want to buy. Can I refer them?**
Yes. Use the **Buyer Referral Program**, under **Indicar compradores** (refer buyers).
