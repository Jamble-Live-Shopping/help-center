# Paying in card installments

On eligible credit card purchases, you can split the payment into installments, when that option is available.

## How to pay in installments

1. When paying, choose your credit card.
2. If the purchase can be split, the installment options show at checkout.
3. Choose the number of installments. The amount of each installment and the installment surcharge show before you confirm.
4. Confirm the payment.

You can also set your installment preference in the app's payment settings.

## When installments are offered

Installments depend on a few conditions:

- **Credit card only.** Pix payments are always paid in full
- **Depends on the seller.** Not every seller offers installments
- **Depends on the purchase amount.** Low-value purchases may not have the option, and each installment has a minimum amount

If installments do not show, the payment is made in full (1x).

## What it costs

Paying in installments has a surcharge, shown in the order summary as **Acréscimo por parcelamento**. You see the total before you confirm. Paying in full has no surcharge.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I pay in installments with Pix?**
No. Installments are only for credit card payments.

**Why don't I see the installment option?**
The seller may not offer installments, or the purchase amount may be too low to split. In those cases, you pay in full.

**Where do I see how much I will pay in total?**
In the order summary, before you confirm the payment.
