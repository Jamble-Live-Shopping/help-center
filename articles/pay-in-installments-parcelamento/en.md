# Paying in card installments

On eligible credit card purchases, you can split the payment into installments.

## How to pay in installments

1. When paying, choose your credit card.
2. If the purchase can be split, the installment options show at checkout.
3. Choose the number of installments. For each option, you see the installment amount, the total and the surcharge compared with paying in full (1x).
4. Confirm the payment.

On your first installment purchases, you always choose and confirm the installments at checkout.

## Your installment preference

In **Settings**, tap **Installments** to set:

- How many installments you prefer, or whether you always prefer to pay in full (1x)
- From which amount you want to choose and confirm the installments at checkout

Your preferences apply to your next purchases, when installments are available. On a computer, the same option is in **My Account**, under **Installments**.

## When installments are offered

Installments depend on a few conditions:

- **Credit card only.** Pix payments are always paid in full
- **Depends on the seller.** Some sellers do not accept installments yet
- **Depends on the purchase amount.** Low-value purchases may not have the option, and each installment has a minimum amount
- **Depends on the card.** Some cards do not accept installments

When installments are not available, checkout shows the reason and the payment is made in full (1x). To see the installment options, use the latest version of the app.

## What it costs

Paying in installments has a surcharge, paid by the buyer. The surcharge grows with the number of installments. Paying in full (1x) has no surcharge.

You see the amount of each installment and the total before you confirm. In the order summary, the surcharge is included in the **Processing fees** line.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I pay in installments with Pix?**
No. Installments are only for credit card payments.

**Why don't I see the installment option?**
The seller may not accept installments yet, the purchase amount may be too low to split, or your card may not accept installments. Checkout shows the reason, and you pay in full (1x).

**Where do I see how much I will pay in total?**
When you choose the installments, each option shows the total. The order summary also shows the final amount before you confirm.

**Who pays the installment surcharge?**
The buyer. Installments have no extra cost for the seller.
