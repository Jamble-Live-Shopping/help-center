# Set Up and Start a Payout

## What you will learn

How to register your payout account on Jamble and withdraw your earnings to your bank account.

## Before you start

You need:

- An approved seller account on Jamble
- A Brazilian bank account in your name, or in your company's name
- Your CPF, or your CNPJ if you sell as a company
- A balance under **Disponível para saque** (available to withdraw) in your wallet

## Step 1: Open your wallet

In the app, open **Ajustes** (settings) and tap **Minha carteira** (my wallet). On a computer, open **Carteira** in the Seller Hub.

## Step 2: Register your payout account (first time only)

The first time, your wallet asks you to set up your payout account. Choose the registration type:

- **CPF**, if you sell as an individual
- **CNPJ**, if you sell as a company (MEI, LTDA and others)

Then fill in your details and bank details in the form. You may be asked for an **identity verification**. If so, follow the on-screen instructions to the end.

You need an active payout account to host lives and to withdraw.

## Step 3: Check your bank details

After registering, your wallet shows your **Dados bancários** (bank details). Check that the name, bank, branch and account are correct. Wrong details make the withdrawal fail.

## Step 4: Withdraw your earnings

When your balance under **Disponível para saque** is above the minimum shown in your wallet, the **Sacar** (withdraw) button becomes active. Tap it and confirm. Your whole available balance is sent to your registered bank account.

The money usually arrives within 5 business days, depending on your bank.

After a withdrawal, you need to wait a little before making another one. The app shows when you can withdraw again.

## Step 5: Follow your withdrawal

Open the **Histórico de saques** (withdrawal history) in your wallet to see each withdrawal, its amount, date and status.

## Withdrawal statuses

- **Criado** (created): the request was received
- **Pendente** (pending): the transfer is being processed
- **Concluído** (completed): the money was sent to your account
- **Falhou** (failed): something went wrong. See the reason on the withdrawal and the troubleshooting article
- **Cancelado** (cancelled): the withdrawal was cancelled

## Key tips

- **Register your payout account before your first live.** You need it to host lives and to withdraw
- **Withdrawing is free.** Withdrawals to your bank account cost nothing
- **Keep your bank details up to date.** If you change banks, update them before withdrawing
- **CPF or CNPJ.** Choose the right type when you register. If you need to switch later, use **Mudar de entidade (CPF/CNPJ)** in your wallet

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I get my money?**
By transfer to your registered bank account. When you tap **Sacar**, your whole available balance is sent to that account.

**How long does a withdrawal take?**
It usually arrives within 5 business days, depending on your bank.

**Is there a minimum?**
Yes. The minimum shows in your wallet. Once your available balance is above it, the **Sacar** button becomes active.

**How often can I withdraw?**
Whenever you have an available balance. After a withdrawal, wait a little: the app shows when you can withdraw again.

**Is there a withdrawal fee?**
No. Withdrawals are free.

**I don't see the Sacar button. Why?**
You do not have an available balance yet, or your payout account is not active. Check your wallet: if a step is pending, such as identity verification, complete it first.

**Can I change my bank account?**
Yes. Update your bank details from your wallet. To switch between CPF and CNPJ, use **Mudar de entidade (CPF/CNPJ)**.

**What if my withdrawal fails?**
Check that your bank details are correct and see the reason shown on the withdrawal. If the problem continues, contact support.
