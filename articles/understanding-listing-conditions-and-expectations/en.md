# Understanding Listing Conditions

## What you will learn

When you create a listing, Jamble asks for the item's **Condição** (Condition). The options change with the category: a Pokémon card is not rated like a piece of clothing or a coin. This guide shows the conditions for each category, what each one means and how to choose the right one.

## Before you start

Have the item in hand and good light to check every detail. Condition is required in every category.

## Where to choose the condition

1. When you create or edit a product, in the app or in the Seller Hub, choose the **Categoria** (Category) first
2. After that, the **Condição** field appears with the options for that category
3. Tap **Condição** and choose the option that describes the item. Each option shows a short description, which the buyer also sees

If you change the category, choose the condition again, because the options may change.

## What each condition means

These are the descriptions shown in the app:

- **Novo (New):** brand new, unused item
- **Novo com etiquetas (New with tags):** unworn and pristine, complete with original purchase tags
- **Novo sem etiquetas (New without tags):** never worn, in perfect condition, but without the original tags
- **Mint:** pristine condition, showing no signs of wear or damage
- **Quase Mint (Near Mint):** almost perfect condition with only very minor signs of wear
- **Pouco Usado (Lightly Played):** shows some signs of wear but still in good condition overall
- **Muito Usado (Heavily Played):** significant wear and tear, with noticeable damage or defects
- **Muito Bem Conservado (Very Fine):** light wear, strong eye appeal, minor surface imperfections
- **Bem Conservado (Fine):** moderate wear with visible signs of use
- **Muito Bom (Very Good):** lightly used with minimal signs of wear, well maintained
- **Bom (Good):** gently worn and well cared for, with any defects clearly noted
- **Razoável (Fair):** heavy wear throughout but still complete
- **Ruim (Poor):** severe wear, major defects, but identifiable
- **Danificado (Damaged):** severely worn or damaged, with major defects that affect usability
- **Flor de Cunho (Uncirculated)** (coins): never entered circulation, full mint luster
- **Soberba (About Uncirculated)** (coins): slight wear on the highest points only
- **Muito Bem Conservada (Extremely Fine)** (coins): light wear on high points, sharp details

## How to choose the right condition

Follow a simple rule: **when in doubt, choose one level lower**. It is better for the buyer to get an item a bit better than expected than a bit worse.

- **Factory sealed product:** use **Novo** when the category has this option. In categories without **Novo**, such as Bonecos and Figurinhas, only use **Mint** if the packaging is also perfect
- **Single cards:** **Mint** and **Quase Mint** are for cards with no visible marks. White edges, scratches or creases call for **Pouco Usado** or **Muito Usado**
- **Boxed cars and figures:** the box condition counts. Dents, tears or a loose blister must show in the photos and the description
- **Clothes and shoes:** only use **Novo com etiquetas** if the tags are still attached

## What to write in the description

The condition is only the summary. The **description** and the **photos** fill in the details. The lower the condition, the more detail you should give:

- Show the item front and back
- Take close-up photos of every defect: scratches, worn edges, dents, stains
- For graded cards, show the grade and the grading service in the photo and fill in the listing fields when the category has them

## Edit the condition after listing

You can change the condition as long as the product has not been sold. Open the product, tap **Editar** (Edit) and save the new condition.

## Important tips

- **Be accurate.** Buyers decide by looking at your photos and the condition you chose. If the item arrives different, they can ask support for help
- **Show the defects.** Transparency avoids doubts and problems after the sale
- **Check the category.** The right condition depends on the right category

## Frequently asked questions

**Why did the condition options change?**
Conditions depend on the category. When you change the category, the options change.

**Can I change the condition after listing?**
Yes, as long as the product has not been sold. Open the product, tap **Editar** and save.

**Which condition do I use for a sealed booster?**
Use **Novo**, which exists in the TCG and Cards Esportivos categories.

**What happens if the buyer says the condition does not match?**
The buyer can contact support. The more accurate your condition, description and photos are, the easier it is to solve.
