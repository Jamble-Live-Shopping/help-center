Once your order is shipped, you can track its progress directly from the app.

How to track your order

- Go to your profile
- Click on “my purchases”
- Select the relevant order from the list
- View shipment details and tracking information

Please note:

- Tracking updates may take time to appear
- Delivery estimates are provided by carriers and may vary

If tracking has not updated for an extended period, you can contact Jamble Support through the App: profile > setting top right > select 'contact support' top of page.
