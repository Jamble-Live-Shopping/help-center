## Buyer-side cancellations

Once an offer is confirmed:

- The purchase becomes binding
- Cancellations are not permitted unless seller agrees before shipping

## Seller-side cancellations

Sellers may not cancel a confirmed purchase because:

- The outcome is unfavorable
- The product value exceeds expectations

Unauthorized cancellations may result in penalties.

Cancellations are only permitted when:

- Required by law
- The product cannot be fulfilled
- Explicitly authorized by Jamble
