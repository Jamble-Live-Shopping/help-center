# Notification Settings

## What you'll learn

This guide explains how to choose which Jamble notifications you get on your phone, so you stay up to date on what matters without too many alerts.

## Before you start

- You need a Jamble account (buyer or seller)
- Jamble notifications must be allowed in your phone settings. If they are turned off, the app shows a message asking you to turn them on

## Step by step

### Step 1: Open the notification settings

In the app, open your **profile** and tap the **gear icon** to open the settings. In the **Conta** (Account) section, tap **Notificações** (Notifications).

### Step 2: Choose what you want to receive

Notifications are organized in four sections. Each section has a main switch that turns everything in that section on or off, plus separate switches for each type of notification.

**Promoções** (Promotional)
- **Alertas promocionais** (Promotional alerts): news about offers, discounts and more

**Atividade** (Activity)
- **Novos seguidores** (Followers): when someone starts following you
- **Mensagens** (Messages): when you receive a message

**Lives** (Live Shows)
- **Lives recomendadas** (Recommended shows): lives you might like
- **Lives de quem eu sigo** (Shows from people I follow): when a seller you follow goes live or schedules a live
- **Lives salvas** (Bookmarked shows): reminders about the lives and items you saved

**Transações** (Transactions)
- **Atualizações sobre o status das transações** (Updates on transaction status): changes to your orders and sales, such as confirmation, shipping and delivery

### Step 3: Turn them on or off

Tap the switch next to each notification to turn it on or off. Turning off a section's main switch turns off every notification in that section at once.

Changes are saved right away. You don't need to tap a save button.

## Important tips

- **Sellers, keep Transações on.** This way you hear about every sale and every order update, and you don't miss shipping deadlines
- **Turn on Lives de quem eu sigo.** You'll know when your favorite sellers go live
- **Turn on Lives salvas.** When you save a live, Jamble can let you know when it starts
- **Settings apply per phone.** If you use Jamble on more than one device, adjust notifications on each one

## Frequently asked questions

**Where do I find the notification settings?**
On your profile, tap the gear icon and, in the **Conta** (Account) section, tap **Notificações** (Notifications).

**I'm not getting notifications. What should I do?**
First, check that Jamble notifications are allowed in your phone settings. Then check in the app that the notifications you want are turned on.

**Can I turn off all notifications at once?**
Yes. Turn off the main switch of each of the four sections.

**If I turn off notifications, will I still see updates in the app?**
Yes. These settings only control the alerts sent to your phone. Your messages, orders and activity keep showing up normally inside the app.

**Do the settings apply to all my devices?**
No. Preferences are saved on each phone. If you use Jamble on two devices, set up each one.
