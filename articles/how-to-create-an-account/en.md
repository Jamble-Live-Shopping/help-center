# How to create an account?

Creating a Jamble account is quick and free. You must be **18 or older**.

## What you will learn

- How to create your account
- What you need to start buying
- How to become a seller later

## Step by step

1. Download the Jamble app from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Open the app and enter your phone number. You can also sign in with Google or, on iPhone, with Apple, under **Outros Métodos de Login** (other login methods).
3. Enter the code you received by WhatsApp or SMS.
4. Complete your details, such as name, CPF and email.
5. If someone invited you to Jamble, use the invite code during sign-up.
6. Pick the categories you like to personalize the lives you see.

By continuing, you agree to Jamble's **Termos de serviço** (Terms of Service) and **Política de Privacidade** (Privacy Policy).

## After you create your account

You can already watch lives, chat and follow sellers. To buy, you also need a shipping address and a payment method. See [Set up your Account to Purchase](https://intercom.help/jamble-bb4bea116bbe/en/articles/10373403).

## I want to sell on Jamble

Once your account is created, tap **Candidate-se para vender ao vivo** (Apply to sell live) on your profile. See [Start Selling on Jamble](https://intercom.help/jamble-bb4bea116bbe/en/articles/14288077).

## Key tips

- Use a phone number that belongs to you. It is used to sign in to your account
- If the code does not arrive, wait a few seconds and ask for a new code in the app
- Your CPF is required to buy and sell, and it cannot be changed once validated
