Creating a Jamble account is quick and easy but you must be over 18 years.

Steps to create an account:

1. Download the Jamble app from the App Store or Google Play
2. Open the app and enter your phone number sign in
3. Verify your account using the SMS code sent to you
4. Accept the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy
5. Add your email address to receive communication

Once your account is created, you can:

- Join live shows
- Set up your account to send offers
- Make purchase on Live shows
- Follow sellers
- Interact with the community

You can choose to become a seller later by completing the [seller application form](https://ask.jambleapp.com/r/mREPyJ).
