# Seller Hub: sell from your computer

The Seller Hub is Jamble for sellers on a computer. In your browser, you can create products, schedule and host your lives, follow your sales, print labels and withdraw your earnings.

It is the same account as the app. What you do on your computer shows up on your phone, and the other way around.

## Who can use it

The Seller Hub is for people who already sell on Jamble. If you are not a seller yet, apply in the app. See [Apply to Sell on Jamble](https://intercom.help/jamble-bb4bea116bbe/en/articles/14288078).

Signing in on the website does not create a new account and does not change anything on your profile.

## How to sign in

1. On your computer, go to **jamble.com/seller/dashboard**.
2. Enter the same phone number as your app account and click **Receber código no WhatsApp** (get the code on WhatsApp). If the code does not arrive, click **Reenviar por SMS** (resend by SMS).
3. Enter the 6-digit code.

If you sign in to the app with Google or Apple, you can also use **Entrar com Google** or **Entrar com Apple**.

## What you find in the menu

- **Visão Geral** (Overview): a summary of your account and your next lives
- **Lives**: schedule, edit and follow your lives
- **Catálogo** (Catalog): all your products, to edit, duplicate or delete
- **Vendas** (Sales): your sales, with the amount and fees of each one
- **Envios** (Shipping): your parcels and labels, plus **Endereços** (addresses), **Perfis de Envio** (shipping profiles) and **Preferências de Envio** (shipping preferences)
- **Notas Fiscais** (invoices): your invoices and your company details
- **Carteira** (Wallet): your balance, withdrawals and the history in **Saques**
- **Indicações** (Referrals): to refer buyers and sellers
- **Meu Perfil** (My profile): your personal details, your moderators and blocked profiles

**Mensagens** (Messages) is the icon at the top of the screen.

## The Criar button

At the top of the screen, the **Criar** (Create) button gathers the most used shortcuts:

- **Agendar Live**: create a new live
- **Criar Novo Produto**: choose **Marketplace** to sell anytime, without a live, or add the product to a live
- **Clonar de Lives Anteriores**: reuse products from past lives
- **Importar CSV**: create many products at once from a spreadsheet

## Import products from a spreadsheet

With the CSV import, you create many products at once. The products go into a scheduled live, so schedule the live first.

1. Click **Criar**, then **Importar CSV**.
2. Click **Baixar Modelo XLSX** (download the template) and open it in Excel or another spreadsheet program.
3. Pick the tab for your products' category and fill in one row per product.
4. Save the file as **CSV**.
5. In the Seller Hub, pick the live under **Importar para a Live** and upload the file.

Before any product is created, the Seller Hub shows a preview. If a row has an error, the preview points out what to fix. Each file can have up to 200 rows and 2 MB. For more products, split them into several files.

## Host your live from your computer

To start a live, you need a payout account and a shipping address on file. If one is missing, the Seller Hub tells you and shows where to set it up.

On your live's page, click **Iniciar Live** (start live) and choose:

- **Transmitir**: the live starts with your computer's camera and microphone
- **Iniciar sem transmitir**: the live starts and you stream from another device, like your phone

During the live, you can switch devices:

- **Transmitir daqui** or **Assumir transmissão** moves the stream to your computer
- **Ficar como controle remoto** keeps the computer for managing products, chat and sales while your phone streams

### Stream with OBS

If you use OBS Studio, the Seller Hub has a step-by-step guide under **Transmitir com OBS**. You do not need a server or a stream key: Jamble uses the OBS Virtual Camera.

- Set the OBS canvas to portrait, 720×1280 at 30 fps
- On Windows, use Chrome. On Mac, use Safari
- Pick your microphone in Jamble, because the Virtual Camera does not carry OBS audio
- Run a private test, with no viewers, before going live

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I sell from my computer?**
Yes. In the Seller Hub you create products, host lives, confirm sales, print labels and withdraw your earnings, all in your browser.

**Do I need to install anything?**
No. The Seller Hub works in your browser. OBS is optional, only if you want to build scenes for your live.

**Does what I do on my computer show up in the app?**
Yes. It is the same account, so products, lives, sales and messages show up on both.

**Can I create an account or apply to sell on the website?**
No. Your account and your application to sell are done in the app. Once approved, you sign in to the Seller Hub with the same account.

**How do I get help?**
In the Seller Hub menu, click **Pedir Ajuda** (get help) to talk to our support team.
