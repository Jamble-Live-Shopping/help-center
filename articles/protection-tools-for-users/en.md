## Reporting a user

- Users can report buyers or sellers when they experience behavior that violates Jamble’s rules or community standards.
- Reports must be based on real and factual issues, such as harassment, fraud, abuse, or rule violations.
- All reports are reviewed carefully by Jamble.

## What happens after a report

- Reports are analyzed by Jamble’s team
- Additional information, screenshots, or recordings may be requested to complete the review
- If a violation is confirmed, Jamble may take appropriate action depending on the severity, frequency, and overall context

Possible actions may include warnings, feature restrictions, suspension, or permanent account removal.

Not all reports result in action, especially when there is insufficient evidence or no rule violation is identified.

---

## Blocking another user

Users may block other users at any time to:

- Prevent direct interactions, including chat messages and live interactions

Blocking does not:

- Cancel existing or confirmed orders
- Affect ongoing transactions or deliveries

Blocking is intended to prevent future interactions, not to resolve disputes.

- as a seller you can select a moderator to help you:
   - delete chat during the show
   - block user in audience

---

## Limits of protection tools

To protect the integrity of the platform:

- Abuse or misuse of reporting tools is not allowed
- Submitting false, misleading, or repetitive reports may result in penalties
- Jamble may restrict access to reporting or other features in cases of abuse

These tools are designed to protect users, not to be used as leverage in disputes or conflicts.
