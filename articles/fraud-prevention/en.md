Jamble actively monitors transactions and payment status to prevent fraud.

## Payment checks

- Payments status are reviewed before confirmation
   - if a payment fail, the buyer cannot purchase on the show anymore (he will be unlocked for future shows)
- Transactions may be delayed or blocked for security reasons

## Buyers at risk

A buyer may be classified as “at risk” based on:

- Buyer haven’t completed 5 transactions
- Payment failures
- Abnormal purchasing patterns
- Excessive refund history
- High volume of similar address on different accounts

Risk classification may result in:

- Black list delivery address
- Lower offer sent limit
- Account restrictions
- Account removal

All actions can be done without prior notice to the user.
