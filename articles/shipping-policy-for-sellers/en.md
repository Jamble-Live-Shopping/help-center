# Shipping Policy for Sellers

## What you will learn

This guide covers Jamble's shipping rules: the deadline to ship after a sale, how to create the label, packaging, tracking and what happens if you are late.

## Before you start

You need:

- An approved seller account on Jamble
- At least one confirmed sale

## The 4-step flow

After a sale, you are responsible for:

1. **Creating the shipping label** from the live's dashboard, from **Envios** (shipments) in the app or in the Seller Hub
2. **Packing the item** properly to avoid damage in transit
3. **Taking the package** to a Correios post office
4. **Following the tracking** until delivery

Jamble shipments go through **Correios**, with the label created in the app or in the Seller Hub.

## Shipping deadline

The shipping deadline is usually about 10 days after the sale is confirmed. In exceptional situations, such as a Correios strike, it can be extended to up to 20 days. The exact deadline always shows on the order, under **Prazo de envio**. Ship as soon as you can, without waiting for the last day.

- **Live sales** arrive already confirmed, so the deadline starts at the time of the sale
- **Loja (shop) sales** need to be confirmed by you within the time shown in the app. The shipping deadline starts when you confirm

## What happens if you are late

If the deadline passes without shipping, the order shows as **Envio atrasado** (late shipment). Ship as soon as you can: after a few days, the sale is cancelled and the buyer is refunded automatically.

Frequent delays also hurt your seller reputation and can lead to account restrictions.

## What the buyer pays

The buyer pays for shipping at checkout. The amount is calculated automatically based on:

- The product's **shipping profile** (package weight and size)
- Your **shipping address** (origin)
- The **buyer's address** (destination)

Shipping is never taken from your earnings.

## How to create the label

After the sale, open the live's dashboard or **Envios**. Sales are organized by buyer, in packages. If the package asks for it, complete the invoice step (**Adicionar NF** or **Isentar NF**) and tap **Adicionar Etiqueta** (add label). From the live's dashboard, you can also create the labels for all packages at once.

Each label includes:

- The sender's and recipient's addresses
- The Correios tracking number and barcode
- The content declaration, when needed

You choose the label format in your **Preferências de envio** (shipping preferences): **Meia página** (half page), **Página inteira** (full page) or **Térmica** (thermal). See [Generate and Print Shipping Labels](https://intercom.help/jamble-bb4bea116bbe/en/articles/14288141).

## Packaging requirements

- **Use packaging that fits** the item's size and weight, matching the product's shipping profile
- **Protect the item** with bubble wrap, paper or other filling
- **Seal it securely** so the package does not open on the way
- **Keep the label visible**, with the barcode and addresses free of folds

See [Packaging Guidelines](https://intercom.help/jamble-bb4bea116bbe/en/articles/14288140).

## Tracking

Every label created by Jamble includes a tracking number. You and the buyer follow the shipment in the app, and tracking updates as the package moves through Correios.

## If the buyer says it did not arrive

1. Check the tracking in the app.
2. If Correios shows the package as delivered, send the buyer a screenshot of the tracking through messages.
3. If the package has been stuck for a long time or was lost, contact our support team to open a review.

Reply quickly. Most cases are solved with a good conversation.

## Cancelling as a seller

If you need to cancel a sale, open the sale in the app, tap **Cancelar venda** (cancel sale) and choose a reason:

- Não foi possível enviar o item (unable to ship the item)
- Item perdido (item lost)
- Item danificado (item damaged)
- O comprador solicitou o cancelamento (buyer requested cancellation)
- Outras razões (other reasons)

The buyer is refunded to the same payment method. Frequent cancellations can lead to account restrictions.

## Invoice (Nota Fiscal)

Before you create the label, the package may ask for the invoice step:

- **Adicionar NF** (add invoice): Jamble can issue the invoice for you, or you enter the key of an invoice you already issued
- **Isentar NF** (exempt invoice): if you do not need to issue an invoice for this sale

You find your invoices under **Notas Fiscais**, in the app and in the Seller Hub.

## Key tips

- **Ship on time.** It is the most important rule
- **Choose the right shipping profile.** It sets the shipping the buyer pays
- **Pack carefully.** Damaged items lead to returns and bad reviews
- **Keep your shipping address up to date** in your shipping preferences
- **Let the buyer know if there is a delay,** through messages

## Frequently asked questions

**Who pays for shipping?**
The buyer. The amount is calculated automatically and shows at checkout.

**Can I use a carrier other than Correios?**
Today, Jamble labels are Correios labels.

**How many days do I have to ship?**
Usually about 10 days after the sale is confirmed, and up to 20 days in exceptional situations, such as a Correios strike. The exact deadline shows on the order, under **Prazo de envio**.

**What if I cannot ship on time?**
The order shows as **Envio atrasado**. Ship as soon as you can: after a few days, the sale is cancelled and the buyer is refunded.

**What if the buyer complains about shipping?**
Shipping depends on the shipping profile, weight, size and distance. Check that the product's shipping profile is correct. If something looks wrong, contact support.

**Do I need to issue a Nota Fiscal?**
It depends on your situation. The app shows the invoice step before the label, with the options to add or exempt.
