# Abuse and sanctions

Jamble takes action against abusive behavior to protect buyers, sellers and the community. These measures apply to both buyers and sellers.

## What you'll learn

- Examples of behavior considered abusive
- Which measures Jamble may apply
- What to do if you disagree with a measure

## Examples of abuse

- Repeatedly winning sales in lives and not paying
- Repeatedly refusing to accept deliveries without a reason
- Sellers who frequently cancel sales
- Manipulating offers or the flow of a live, for example with fake or coordinated accounts
- Trying to move purchases, payments or contacts outside Jamble
- Harassment, threats, insults or inappropriate content
- False or bad faith refund requests
- Sending repeated messages to support without a real request, or false reports

## Possible measures

Depending on how serious and how frequent it is, Jamble may apply:

- Removal of messages or content
- Being blocked from a live
- A temporary pause on purchases, with the **Compras pausadas** (purchases paused) message and the date when you can buy again
- Temporary restriction of account features
- Suspension of access to lives
- Account suspension or closure

Measures may be applied without prior notice when needed to protect the community. You may be informed in the app, by notification or through other channels.

## If you disagree with a measure

Contact support through the in-app chat, explain the situation and send anything that can help the review, such as screenshots.
