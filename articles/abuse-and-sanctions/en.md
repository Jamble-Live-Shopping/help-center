Jamble enforces strict measures against abusive behavior.

## Examples of abuse

- Repeated cancellations or refusal to accept delivery
- Seller cancellations after favorable outcomes
- Manipulating offers or live dynamics
- Harassment, threats, or inappropriate content
- Persistant contact to support requesting refunds
- Persistant contact to support with empty request

## Possible sanctions

Depending on severity and frequency:

- Temporary feature restrictions (mute, disable, banned)
- Live access suspension
- Account suspension or permanent ban

Sanctions may apply to buyers and sellers and are not necessarily communicated by email.
