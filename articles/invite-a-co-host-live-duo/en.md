# Invite a co-host (Live Duo)

## What you'll learn

How to invite another person to appear in your live with **Live Duo**, what the guest sees, what they can do, and how to end their participation. Live Duo works on iPhone, on Android, and in the live on your computer (Seller Hub).

## Before you start

- An approved seller account on Jamble
- Your live must be on air. The invite only shows while the live is running
- The person you invite needs to be in your live to accept. If they are not, they get a notification to join

## How Live Duo works

With Live Duo, the guest's camera and microphone join your broadcast. Your camera stays as the main picture and the guest shows in a smaller window. Your audience sees and hears both of you.

You can have **one guest at a time**. To bring in someone else, remove the current guest first.

## Step 1: open the invite

With the live on air, tap the camera icon with the plus sign in the controls at the top right. The **Convite para uma dupla** (Invite for a Duo) screen opens (on Android, **Convide para um Duo Live**).

![Invite for a Duo screen with the Search for a friend bar, the live's viewers, and the Invite your friend button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Jamble-Live-Shopping/help-center/main/assets/mockups/invite-a-co-host-live-duo__invite-bottom-sheet__en__v4.png)

The list shows who is watching now. You can also search for anyone by username. Tap the person, then **Convide seu amigo** (Invite your friend; on Android, **Convidar**).

Another way: tap a viewer's name in the chat and choose **Convidar como convidado** (Invite as guest).

## Step 2: the guest accepts

The guest sees the **Live Duo** screen with the invite.

![Live Duo screen seen by the guest with the invite message and the Accept and Join and Don't Join buttons](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Jamble-Live-Shopping/help-center/main/assets/mockups/invite-a-co-host-live-duo__guest-invite-modal__en__v4.png)

- To join, they tap **Aceitar e participar** (Accept and join; on Android, **Aceitar e Entrar**). If the app doesn't have camera and microphone access yet, it asks for permission first
- To decline, they tap **Não participe** (Don't join; on Android, **Depois**)

If the invite isn't accepted within a few minutes, it lapses and you can invite again.

## Step 3: live together

When the guest accepts, their camera joins the live and their photo shows in your controls, in place of the invite icon.

## Remove the guest

1. Tap the guest's photo in the live controls
2. Confirm with **Remover** (Remove) in the **Remover (name) do Live Duo?** (Remove name from Live Duo?) alert (on Android, **Remover (name) do Duo Live**)

![Remove from Live Duo alert with the Cancel and Remove buttons](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Jamble-Live-Shopping/help-center/main/assets/mockups/invite-a-co-host-live-duo__remove-guest-alert__en__v4.png)

You can also tap their name in the chat and choose **Remover convidado** (Remove guest). They go back to being a regular viewer.

The guest can also leave on their own. They tap their own broadcast button and confirm with **Parar** (Stop).

## Live Duo on your computer

In the live on your computer, in the Seller Hub, use **Convidar para Live Duo** (Invite to Live Duo) to bring in someone from your audience. To finish, use **Encerrar Live Duo** (End Live Duo). The guest goes off air and back to being a viewer, and you can invite again later.

## What the guest can and can't do

- **Can**: appear with camera and audio, talk in the chat, leave the broadcast whenever they want
- **Can't**: sell, manage products, end the live, or invite other people

Products, sales, and live controls stay with you only.

## Important tips

- **Anyone can be invited.** They don't need to be a seller or follow you
- **Agree beforehand.** Tell the guest to allow camera and microphone on their phone
- **Ask them to join the live.** People already watching get the invite right away
- **No invite icon?** Check that the live is on air and that there is no active guest

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the guest need to be a seller?**
No. Any Jamble user can be invited.

**Can I have more than one guest?**
No. Live Duo takes one guest at a time.

**Can I invite someone before the live starts?**
No. The invite is only available while the live is on air.

**Can the guest sell during the live?**
No. Only you manage products and sales.

**What if the guest's connection drops?**
Their window disappears from your screen. When they come back to the live, you can invite them again.
