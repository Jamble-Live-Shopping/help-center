Completing your account setup helps ensure smooth transactions and access to all Jamble features.

## Recommended setup steps

- Add a profile picture
- Make sure your email is correctly written (e.g. name@surname.com)
- Validate your CPF in the settings
- Add a detailed shipping address
   - add it through your location, suggestion or manually
   - you can add several, edit and delete shipping addresses at any time
   - your selected address by default will be used to ship your orders
- Add a payment method
   - you can pay by credit card or by Pix
   - when you add a new card, your bank may ask you to confirm it for security
   - for some purchases, the only option available may be Pix
   - you can add, edit and remove cards at any time
- Enable notifications to stay informed about live shows and order updates

You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you provide. If incorrect or incomplete address details are submitted, any resulting delivery issues may not be eligible for a refund.
