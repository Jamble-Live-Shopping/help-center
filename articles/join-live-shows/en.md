## Live shows are the core of the Jamble experience.

How to join a live show:

- Open the Jamble app
- On the home page
   - see the current live on top of page
      - tag “Live” with audience count when show has started
   - Scroll down to see upcoming shows
      - tag day and time informing about the time Live start
- Tap on the show cover to join
- Watch the live stream and interact in real time using the chat

During a live show, you can:

- Send offers when product sales start
- See others purchase confirmation
   - highest offer sent before end of the timer
   - for “buy now” offers, there is no timer, first comes first served system
- Send chat in the show to talk with all the viewers and seller
- Access the shop to see upcoming listed items for sale
- React using in-app interactions accessible from the “gems icon” bottom right
- Display shows subtitles
- Share show to friends
- Redeem a code
- Access to help center
- Report technical issue
- Join a battle, purchase and send stickers to earn points
- Collect rewards (if applicable)
- Manage payment method, shipping address through “billing details”

Important information:

- Offers and purchases are final to keep auctions fair for everyone
- For pack opening products, buyer can choose their preferences
   - open pack → no cancellations and refunds
   - don’t open pack → cancellations and refunds may apply depending on the context
- Some shows may have specific conditions communicated by the seller
