Jamble adopts strict fraud prevention and risk management practices, aligned with best practices from leading marketplaces and Brazilian market requirements, to protect buyers, sellers, and the integrity of the platform.

## Payment security controls

- Payments may be reviewed before confirmation
- Some transactions may be temporarily held for additional verification
- Payments may be blocked if fraud risk, inconsistencies, or misuse are detected

These controls help reduce chargebacks, fraud, and payment failures, ensuring a safer environment for everyone.

## Buyer risk assessment

Jamble continuously evaluates user behavior to prevent fraudulent activity, including:

- Excessive refund history
- Failed or suspicious payment attempts
- Unusual or inconsistent purchasing patterns
- Repeated use of multiple accounts with the same delivery or payment details

These factors may increase the risk level associated with a buyer’s account.

## Account restrictions

Based on the identified risk level, Jamble may apply proportional measures such as:

- Purchase or offer limits
- Restricted access to certain features
- Temporary account suspension
- Permanent account suspension in severe or repeated fraud cases

These actions may be applied without prior notice, with the goal of protecting the community.

## Legal compliance

Jamble’s fraud prevention practices comply with Brazilian financial and consumer protection regulations.

When legally required, Jamble may share information with competent authorities, financial institutions, or antifraud partners for investigation and regulatory compliance.

These measures help ensure a safer, more trustworthy, and sustainable ecosystem for all platform users.
