To purchase items on Jamble, you need to have an activated account:

- valid CPF
- valid payment method
- valid shipping address

---

### Valid CPF

To purchase and make sales on Jamble, users must have registered their CPF as required by the law.

Users can enter his CPF :

- during the onboarding steps (for account created after 16/01/26)
- in the settings under personal information

> User cannot edit the CPF once added and validated

---

## Payment Method

Jamble provides secure in-app payments to ensure a safe purchasing experience for buyers and sellers.

- All payments are processed securely within the Jamble App
- Payment information is encrypted and never shared with sellers
- Jamble does not store full payment card details

Once you confirm an offer or dynamic purchase, the payment is authorized according to the transaction rules displayed in the app.

If a payment fails or is declined, you may be prompted to update your payment method.

### Accepted payment methods

- You can add one or more payment methods to your account to complete purchases during live shows or listings.
- Credit cards and digital wallet

> Pix Payment and Installment are not included for now (27/01/26)

---

### Shipping Address

A valid shipping address is required to complete a purchase on Jamble.

You can manage your shipping address in your account settings:

- Add a new address
- Edit an existing address
- Select a default address for future purchases

Make sure your address is:

- Complete and accurate
   - street number, street name, postal code, apt number.
- Accessible for the selected shipping method
- Located in a supported delivery area

> Users are responsible for providing correct shipping information

Users can edit their shipping during the show:

- when they see the confirmation modal
   - displayed once a day at the first purchase of the day
- in the transaction card (activity > purchases) if order is not shipped

---

### Sending offers

Once a user’s account is activated, they can slide the purple call-to-action (CTA) to place an offer and purchase an item.

When the seller starts the sale, the product becomes visible to the audience. Buyers can then:

- Slide right to send an offer at the starting price set by the seller
- Each new offer increases the price by +5 / +10 / +25 / +50, depending on the current price tier

Each time a user sends an offer:

- The bid amount is automatically increased
- +5 seconds are added to the countdown timer (except for Sudden Death and Buy Now formats)

The winner is the user who placed the highest and most recent offer before the timer ends.
