Each purchase on Jamble has a clear order status so you can track its progress.

### Common order statuses include:

- **Confirmed** – Your payment went through and seller confirmed the order after the show. The seller is preparing your order and has up to 8 days to ship it.
- **In Delivery–** The seller shipped your package to your destination, you can follow through the tracking link in your order detail from your app.
- **Delivered** – The buyer received order, pick up validated through shipping provider
- **Completed** – The buyer completed order after reception, automatically done after 3 days.
- **Refunded** - The order has been cancelled and refunded if applicable
- **Investigation -** Either the buyer or seller contacted the customer support about an issue

Order statuses are updated automatically as the transaction progresses.
