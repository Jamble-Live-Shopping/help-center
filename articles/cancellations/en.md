Cancellations on Jamble are governed by the order status, the stage of fulfillment, and applicable legal obligations.

### General policy

Once a purchase is confirmed, it becomes binding. Cancellations are not guaranteed and are evaluated based on the product opening, order status at the time of the request.

A cancellation may be processed in the following situations:

- The seller is unable to fulfill the order
- The seller doesn't ship order within 15 days after confirmation
- The order is stuck in delivery since more than 30 days
- The cancellation is required by applicable consumer protection laws
   - if product is opened (like a pack opened), no cancellation
   - if product is sealed (pack not open) we can cancel the order
      - if request > 7 days and order is not shipped yet
      - if request > 7 days and order is shipped
         - buyer cannot pick the order, package is returned to sender and we cancel when seller receives order

---

### A cancellation request may be refused in the following cases:

- The request is > 7 days after order Created
- The order has already been Completed
- The product has been opened (considered to be used)
- The order has been marked as Delivered by the carrier
- The buyer requests cancellation due to dissatisfaction

Jamble reserves the right to refuse cancellation requests that do not meet the above conditions.
