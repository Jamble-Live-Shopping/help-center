To promote responsible purchasing and platform safety, Jamble may apply custom offers limits.

## Purpose

Maximum offer limits help:

- Securing an offer when the buyer is ready to pay the amount
- Offering flexibility to buyers willing to increase by his preferred amount
- Reduce fraud and payment risk

## How custom offer limits work

- Custom offer must be above current offered price
- Once a custom offer is sent, the buyer is guarantee to get this product at this price until someone else place an offer
   - *starting price was R$5*
   - *current price R$10, user A is currently winning offers*
   - *user A place a custom offer to $R20 to make sure he gets the product*
   - 3 scenarios
      - nobody place an offer, user A gets offer at $R10
      - user B place an offer at R$15, user A is winning the offers to R$16
      - user C places offer at $R21 (above user A max offer), user A loses the offer until he increase his.

Jamble reserves the right to adjust or enforce limits at any time.
