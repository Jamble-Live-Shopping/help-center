## Automated moderation

- Detection of spam or abusive behavior
   - Spamming or trolling the chat
   - Harassing, insulting, or provoking other users
   - Selling or attempting to sell unacceptable or prohibited inventory
   - Displaying inappropriate or explicit body parts during a Live
   - Sharing misleading, false, or deceptive information
   - Disrupting the normal flow of a live show on purpose
   - Talk about political topics
   - Talk about any topic against Human Right
   - Promoting personal business outside the platform
- Identification of suspicious activity
   - Repeated or coordinated spam messages
   - Unusual behavior patterns across multiple accounts
   - Attempts to bypass platform rules or safety systems
   - Activity that suggests fraud, manipulation, or abuse

## Human moderation

- Manual review of reported users
- Enforcement of live show rules

Attempts to bypass moderation systems, including the use of bots or automated tools, are strictly prohibited.
