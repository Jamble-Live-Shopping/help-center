# New Seller Guide to Listing Products

## What you will learn

A quick guide to creating your first listings on Jamble, with tips to sell more. The full step-by-step for each field is in [How to List Products on Jamble](https://intercom.help/jamble-bb4bea116bbe/en/articles/14288093).

## Where to create your listings

- **In a live:** open your scheduled live and add your products
- **In the Loja (shop):** listings that are for sale at any time, without a live
- **On a computer:** in the Seller Hub, including from a spreadsheet (**Importar CSV**)

## The basics of each listing

1. **Title:** up to 40 characters, clear and specific
2. **Category and condition:** the category is required, and the condition options change by category
3. **Photos:** of the real item you will ship
4. **Sell mode:** Oferta em tempo real (real-time offer), Morte súbita (sudden death) or Comprar agora (buy now)
5. **Price:** from R$ 5
6. **Shipping profile:** the weight and size of the finished package

## Titles that sell

**Good examples:**

- "Charizard VMAX Holo PSA 10"
- "Booster Pokémon TCG Escarlate e Violeta Lacrado"

**Avoid:**

- "Card" (too vague)
- "Beautiful rare card amazing deal!!!" (does not say what it is)

## Photos that sell

- Use good lighting. Natural light near a window works well
- Show the item from several angles: front, back and, for cards, the corners
- Show close-ups of details, signatures, the grading label (PSA, CGC, BGS) and any defect
- Use a clean, simple background
- For diecast, photograph the packaging and the markings on the base

## Sealed products opened live

On sealed products, like boosters, you can turn on **Abertura ao Vivo** (live opening). After paying, the buyer chooses whether the item is opened during the live or shipped sealed. If they do not answer, the item stays sealed.

## Key tips

- **Be honest about the condition.** If there is a defect, mention it and show it in the photo. This avoids returns
- **Start with a few products.** 5 to 10 well-presented items make a good first live
- **Edit or clone your listings.** You can edit a listing before it sells and clone listings to create similar products faster
- **Check the rules.** See the [Listing Guidelines](https://intercom.help/jamble-bb4bea116bbe/en/articles/14288106)

## Frequently asked questions

**Are photos required?**
Not on every listing, but they make a big difference. Buyers make many more offers on items they can see well.

**What is the minimum price?**
R$ 5.

**Can I change a listing after creating it?**
Yes, as long as the product has not been sold.

**What is Abertura ao Vivo?**
It is an option for sealed products. After paying, the buyer chooses whether the item is opened during the live or shipped sealed.
