# 1. How do I become a seller on Jamble?

To start selling, go to your profile and select "Apply to Sell Live" or in your settings by selecting "Apply to Go Live". You'll need to complete a quick application, and once approved and your bank details registered, you can create a Live.

# 2. How do I schedule a live show ?

If you don't have any show created, on your profile, click on"schedule a live show"

If you have already some live shows created and want to add more, on your profile, click on "+" and select "schedule a live show".

Tips for an attractive Live:

- show cover and title
- suited date and time
- creation in advance so users can bookmark your show

# 3. How do I list items in my show?

When you create your show, we can easily add listing through "quick upload":

1. Tap "Quick Upload"
2. Select 1 picture
3. Choose a title
4. Find an accurate description
5. Select product type (real-time offer, sudden death or giveaway)
6. Select starting price (if applicable)
7. Select offer timer (if applicable)
8. Set shipping format (light or heavy pack)
9. Click to Add listing

# 3. What are the fees for selling on Jamble?

Jamble charges a **14% commission** on sales. Some sellers may qualify for promotions with 0% commission for a limited time.

# 4. How do real time offers work?

Offers allow you to sell items in real-time in a limited period of time set when you list item. Once a seller starts the sale of an item, buyers send offers until timer is at 0s left.

When countdown is at 0s, the highest offer wins the item and Jamble charges the buyer's card to create a transaction.

Do not start next sale until payment is confirmed, as if payment fails, you will have to re run the product.

# 6. How do I get paid for my sales?

Payments are processed through Pagar.me. You'll receive your earnings directly to your bank account within 3 days after order is confirmed by the buyer.

# 7. How does shipping work for sellers?

Buyers cover shipping costs and depends on bundle weights.

Many item can enter a box, depending the weight.

Sellers are responsible for:

- confirming sale
- selecting invoice preference
- generating shipping label
- shipping items within 8 days

# 8. Can I cancel a sale as a seller?

Yes in the order details you can click to "cancel" but should do it only if absolutely necessary. Frequent cancellations may lead to account restrictions.

# 9. How do I handle a dispute with a buyer?

If a buyer reports an issue concerning an order, Jamble Customer Support will step to resolve it and may write you directly. For quicker resolution, always provide accurate descriptions and pictures.

# 10. How can I grow my audience and increase sales?

- Go Live often to engage with buyers
- Promote your shows on your social networks
- Create show in advance (pictures, prices, description)

# 11. How do I contact Jamble support?

From your profile > open your setting > select "contact customer support"
