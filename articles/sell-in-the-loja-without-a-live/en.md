# Sell in the Loja, without a live

In the Loja (shop), you sell your products at any time, at a fixed price, without going live. Your listings stay for sale until they sell out or you delete them.

## Where buyers find your listings

- In the **Loja** tab of the app
- In search, on the products tab
- On your profile

## How to create a listing

![Seller profile with the + button open showing Schedule a Live and Create a Store listing](./assets/mockups/sell-in-the-loja-without-a-live__create-menu__en.png)

**In the app:**

1. Open your profile and tap the **+** button.
2. Choose **Criar anúncio na Loja** (create a shop listing).
3. Fill in the listing and tap **Criar Anúncio**.

**On a computer (Seller Hub):**

1. Click the **+**, then **Criar Novo Produto**.
2. Under **Onde você quer vender?** (where do you want to sell), choose **Marketplace**.
3. Fill in the listing and save.

## What to fill in

- **Photos, title and description**
- **Category** and **condition** of the item
- **Preço** (price): what the buyer pays for the product
- **Preço de Varejo** (retail price, optional): the item's usual price, shown crossed out next to your price
- **Quantidade** (quantity): how many units you have for sale
- **Perfil de envio** (shipping profile): the parcel's weight and size, used to calculate shipping

To sell several identical units, set the **Quantidade**. If a listing sells out, you can raise the quantity to put it back on sale.

## Edit or delete a listing

Open your profile and go to your listings. Open a listing to edit it, or delete it when you want to take it out of the Loja. In the Seller Hub, you do the same under **Catálogo**.

## Offers from buyers

![Pending offer in Messages with the product, the price, the offered amount and the Decline, Counter and Accept buttons](./assets/mockups/sell-in-the-loja-without-a-live__offer-received__en.png)

On Loja listings, buyers can tap **Fazer oferta** (make an offer) and propose a price below yours. The app shows the minimum accepted.

When you get an offer, it shows up in your **Mensagens** (messages). You can:

- **Aceitar** (accept): the buyer is notified and has up to 2 days to pay at checkout
- **Recusar** (decline): the offer is closed
- **Contrapor** (counter, called **Contraproposta** on Android): you propose another price to the buyer

An offer with no answer expires after 3 days. Each buyer can make a limited number of offers on the same product.

## When someone buys

1. **Confirm the sale.** Open **Envios** (shipments) in the app or in the Seller Hub and tap **Revisar vendas pendentes** (review pending sales). For each sale, choose **Confirmar** or **Recusar**. If you decline, the buyer is refunded.
2. **Do it within the time shown in the app.** If the sale is not confirmed in time, it is cancelled and the buyer is refunded automatically.
3. **Create the label and ship.** Once confirmed, you create the label like for any other sale. The shipping deadline starts when you confirm, and the date shows on the order under **Prazo de envio**.

If a buyer takes several of your products in the same purchase, they are shipped together.

## Fees

The standard 10% commission also applies to Loja sales. Shipping is paid by the buyer. See [Jamble Seller Fees and Commissions](https://intercom.help/jamble-bb4bea116bbe/en/articles/14288149).

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to be live to sell in the Loja?**
No. Loja listings are for sale all the time, even when you are not live.

**Can the buyer cancel the purchase?**
Yes, as long as you have not confirmed the sale yet. After you confirm, the order moves on to shipping.

**Do live sales need confirmation too?**
Yes, but in a different way. Live sales are confirmed all together on the live dashboard, with **Confirmar Vendas** (Confirm sales), after the live ends. In the Loja, a purchase can happen at any time, so you confirm or decline each sale separately.
