How payments are processed on Jamble?

- Payments are made directly in the Jamble App
- Jamble collects the payment on behalf of the seller
- Payment confirmation is required to complete a purchase

When a refund may be granted?

- Refunds depend on the order status
- Refunds may be granted if the product is not delivered
- Refunds may be granted if the seller cannot fulfill the order
- Refunds are granted when order is cancelled

When a refund may be refused?

- Orders marked as “Delivered” are not refundable
- Opened or used products may not be refundable
- Refunds may be refused in case of abusive behavior

How refunds are issues?

- Refunds are issued in Jamble Coins by default
   - Jamble Coins do not expire
- Bank transfer refunds may be available under specific conditions
   - user is responsible to share the accurate data information
   - mistake in date information may lead to delays
   - user is also responsible to have a valid KYC
