# Recebimento Antecipado (early payout) and installments

With Recebimento Antecipado, you get the money from card sales faster. It also lets your buyers pay for card purchases in installments, when that option is available.

## How it works

- **Without Recebimento Antecipado:** card payments can take up to 30 days to be released
- **With Recebimento Antecipado:** card payments are released in about 15 days
- **Pix payments** do not change: they are usually released in about 1 day

A sale's amount becomes available to withdraw when the payment is released and the sale is completed. See [How you get paid](https://intercom.help/jamble-bb4bea116bbe/en/articles/14288155).

## What it costs

There is an additional fee of about 1%, applied **only to card payments**. Sales paid with Pix do not have this fee.

## Installments for your buyers

When Recebimento Antecipado is on, your buyers can pay for eligible card purchases in installments. The number of installments and the surcharge are shown to the buyer before they pay.

## How to turn it on

![My Wallet screen with the available balance, the Withdraw button and the Early Payout card with its explanation and toggle](./assets/mockups/early-payout-recebimento-antecipado__wallet-card__en.png)

1. Open your wallet in the app, or **Carteira** (wallet) in the Seller Hub.
2. Tap **Recebimento Antecipado** and follow the instructions.

**Heads up:** this cannot be undone. Once you turn it on, Recebimento Antecipado stays on for your account.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Recebimento Antecipado required?**
No. It is optional. Without it, you keep getting paid as usual, on the standard timelines.

**Does the fee apply to Pix sales?**
No. The fee of about 1% only applies to card payments.

**Can I turn it off later?**
No. Turning on Recebimento Antecipado cannot be undone.

**Can my buyers only pay in installments if I turn it on?**
Yes. Card installments are only offered to buyers of sellers who have Recebimento Antecipado on.
